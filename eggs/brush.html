brush

a spectrum is a picture. paint one. narrow strokes sing, wide strokes hiss, and the width of the brush is the whole difference between a note and a noise. then play the painting from a keyboard: every key slides the whole picture so the marked fundamental lands on that pitch.

paint on the canvas · height is loudness · left is low
brush width 150¢
fundamental (guide) 110 Hz
volume
C4

the strip is the computer keyboard: press the letter on a key, or tap it · tab swaps layouts (cubase: white keys q to ], black on the number row · tracker: z-row and q-row, two octaves) · arrows: left and right shift the octave, up and down set velocity ·

the same brush width is 50¢ per sine everywhere, but 50¢ is 1.6 Hz at the bottom and 200 Hz at the top. so a wide stroke beats slowly in the bass and buzzes in the treble.
