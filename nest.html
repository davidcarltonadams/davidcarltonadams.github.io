nest

a map of the eggs. each one was scored along eight conceptual dimensions — ratio, rhythm, psychoacoustics, illusion, process, visible math, playability, score — and the eight-dimensional space was flattened to this plane. distance is kinship; the layout was found, not designed. hollow eggs are unhatched: gaps the space itself begs for. this map is always slightly out of date, which is how you know the nest is alive.
