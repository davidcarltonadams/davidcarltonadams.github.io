2022 · string quartet · ~9'30"

video: Rothko Quartet

Back in the mid to late 2010’s, I started playing around with the idea of using Just Intonation as the basis for a musical scale. I’ve used elements of this in various works since, but until this year I had never worked with players so ideally suited to this manner of writing.
