in the Rain

2022 · laptop trio · ~8'

trio for laptopists (Max), contact mics, and dice

A quiet yet stormy ambience emerges from samples of rain and granular synthesis. Just as we filter through quotidian commotion to find our own meaning, tones filter through the noise. What emerges is a whistling melody, perhaps the wind… And just as we read whatever signs we choose to see in the world to help us find our next steps, the dice offer guidance to the tones… whistling in the wind…

premiere

2022-02-01 · Cohen-Davidson Theater, Peabody Institute, Baltimore, MD
