flicker

a polyrhythm and an interval with the same ratio are the same phenomenon at different speeds · spin 3 against 2 fast enough and the rhythm fuses into a perfect fifth · henry cowell called it in 1930

 
 
 

below ~8 pulses a second you count a rhythm · past ~30 the pulses fuse and the ratio becomes pitch · the numbers on the label never change — only how fast you are listening
