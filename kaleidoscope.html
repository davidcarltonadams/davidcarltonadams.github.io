Kaleidoscope
2026 · SuperCollider synthesis · live electronics · dance
Composer / Electronics / Viola — David Carlton Adams
Choreographer / Director — Zoë Brielle Payne (ZBRI)
Performers — Josette, Dallas, Anna
Charm City Fringe Festival Spring 2026 · The Peale, Baltimore

program note

Kaleidoscope unfolds over 44 minutes of continuous sound, its formal architecture governed by golden-ratio proportions — a structure that mirrors the natural recursion of the work's sonic materials. SuperCollider synthesis generates an evolving field of timbres drawn from granular processing, spectral manipulation, and microtonal tuning systems. Live viola traces melodic lines through this field, sometimes leading, sometimes dissolving into it.

The dance is not illustration of the music, nor the music illustration of the dance. Each follows its own logic. The collision — the interference pattern — is the work.

Recordings from the Charm City Fringe run are in production — audio will return here on release.
