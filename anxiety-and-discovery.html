Anxiety and Discovery

2021 · electric guitars, effects · ~5'

for electric guitars and effects

This work signified a new degree of integration between what have thus far been somewhat separate facets of my musical life: “rock” and “classical.”

It uses a graphic score that provides a human idea (that of emergent awareness of some unconscious conflict) and a natural shape (based loosely on the golden mean).

This work should be understood as a first step into a larger world, one I have begun to explore further, but for which there remains a dearth of documentation.

premiere

2021-12-03 · Peabody Institute, Baltimore, MD
