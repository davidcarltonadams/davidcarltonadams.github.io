About

Composer, performer, and educator working at the intersection of experimental composition, rock music, and live electronics. I'm completing a DMA at Peabody Institute (Johns Hopkins), with an expected degree conferral of May 2026.

My music draws on a decade of work as a rock guitarist and recording engineer, a certificate in vocal performance, and doctoral training in microtonality, extended form, and electronics. The result is work that moves between acoustic chamber music, electroacoustic composition, and guitar-driven performance — sometimes in the same piece.

Recent compositions include works performed and premiered by Talea Ensemble, Wet Ink Ensemble, Ensemble Linea, JACK Quartet, Friction Quartet, and Old Bay New Music. I'm also a co-founder of the New Uncertainty Collective, an experimental music label and concert series based in Baltimore.

As an educator, I've taught the full theory sequence at Peabody (Theory 1–6), musicianship and composition at The Walden School, and Musicianship 2–3 at Towson University. I care about meeting students where they are — whether they're reading Ligeti scores or writing their first songs.

My background in chemical and nuclear engineering shapes how I think about compositional systems, tuning, and instrument design. I build and perform with Pedal Stool, a custom guitar-effects meta-instrument that can transform almost any sound source in real time.

I'm actively seeking faculty positions in composition and/or music theory for Fall 2026.

Pedal Stool and the SALLT Ensemble

2023 – Present

Pedal Stool is a custom guitar-effects meta-instrument designed and built for real-time transformation of diverse sound sources — electric guitar, voice, synthesizer, or virtually anything else. The SALLT Ensemble is the performance context for this instrument, featured in the April 2026 spring residency finale at Le Mondo Arts in Baltimore. The instrument reflects an ongoing interest in building new tools for live electronic performance.

A chamber work for violin, cello, and fretless electric guitar, premiered at The Walden School in the summer of 2024. The piece explores alternative tunings and the expressive possibilities of the fretless guitar as a voice within a classical string texture.
