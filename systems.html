how it is built

  • Gate the irreversible, log the reversibleoutbound keeps a human yes/no; internal writes are logged and replayable
  • The paper always publishesdaily surfaces fall back to a validated deterministic base
  • Untrusted in, summarized outemail, scraped feeds, and agent output are read-only holds until a human acts
